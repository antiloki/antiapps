How it works

Three steps. No new account.

01

Plug the pendrive in

antiDrive runs on Windows and Linux, from the stick itself. Plug it into any internet-connected PC.

02

Open a URL and a password

From any other machine, open the address and enter the password. There is no account to create, and nothing to forward on your router.

03

Stream from the stick

Files stream straight off the pendrive, with seeking, and pass end-to-end encrypted through a small self-hosted relay. Nobody's cloud holds them.

What runs today

Honest about where it is.

  • Portable on Windows and Linux. It runs from the pendrive.
  • Drive-style UI, built with Rust, Tauri and SQLite
  • Streaming with seeking. Files stay on the stick.
  • End-to-end encrypted relay, Argon2 password, no port forwarding
  • Alpha 0.1.1. Downloads for Windows and Linux are up
