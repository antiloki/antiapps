How to use antiHunt.

Stop hunting leads. Harvest them.

01

Overview

antiHunt finds people who are already asking for what you sell, and helps you answer them. It has two parts:

  • The desktop app keeps your leads in lead packs (SQLite files) on your machine. You open a pack and ask about it in plain language, through the AI CLI you are already signed in to.
  • The browser extension finds leads on YouTube and X inside your own signed-in browser, with a screenshot of each original post or comment.

Outreach is drafts only. The app never sends; you do.

antiHunt is a private, paid beta. Access is by invitation.

02

Install

Invited users get a Linux build: one file. Run it and the chat opens at http://127.0.0.1:8787/chat. It serves on 127.0.0.1 only.

Android, Windows and macOS builds follow. planned

The extension

During the beta the Chrome extension is loaded unpacked: open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked and pick the extension folder you were sent. A Chrome Web Store listing comes later. planned

03

Quick start

  1. Open the app. The free 10-lead sample pack is included.
  2. Drop a purchased pack (.db) on the chat, or choose it with Open pack. It is registered on your machine.
  3. Ask in plain language, for example “who asked about pricing this week?”.
  4. Read the drafted message, change what you like, and send it yourself.

04

Choosing your AI

Natural-language turns run on a CLI you already use: Codex, Claude Code, Gemini, Grok or Cursor's agent. Sign in to it as usual. No API key is stored in the app.

05

Finding leads with the extension

The extension works through the sites you are already signed in to. It never reads your cookies or passwords.

  • YouTube and X: it finds people who ask for what you sell.
  • Filtering: webinar ads, link spam and copy-paste accounts are dropped before they reach your list.
  • Proof: a screenshot of the original post or comment sits in each lead's detail.
  • DM badge: leads whose X messages are open get a green DM badge next to the reach score.

Instagram, LinkedIn (identity checks only) and Gmail are next. planned

06

Scores and drafts

Each lead is scored hot, warm or cold from what the person wrote, and an AI check confirms whether it is really a lead.

Drafts refer to what the person wrote, answer in their language, and never quote prices or numbers that are not on your own site.

Planned outreach flow planned: you approve, edit or skip each draft in the extension's side panel or the app, and only then does the extension post it from your own account. At most one follow-up, after 4 days, with the same approval. Anyone who replies “stop” or “not interested” goes on a do-not-contact list across every platform.

07

Safe pacing

The extension acts slowly and like a person, in visible tabs, and pauses when a site shows a warning.

Planned limits planned: daily caps per platform, random delays between actions, active hours only, a gentler first week, and a 24-hour pause on any CAPTCHA or “unusual activity” page.

Automating a site can break its terms and put your account at risk, even at low volume. Use a dedicated account if you are unsure.

08

Export and MCP

csv-export writes your leads for spreadsheets. mcp lets your own agents query lead packs over MCP. antiPost can also import antiHunt leads read-only.

09

Data and privacy

Lead packs are SQLite files on your computer and the app serves on 127.0.0.1 only. Each contact keeps its source URL. Platform logins never leave your browser.

10

YouTube and X in the extension. Others are planned.
