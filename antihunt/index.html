How it works

Three steps. No new account.

01

Open a pack

Start with the free 10-lead sample pack. Drop a purchased pack (.db) on the chat, or choose it with Open pack, and it is registered on your machine.

02

Ask in plain language

Natural-language turns stream a host CLI you already use: Codex, Claude Code, Gemini, Grok or Cursor's agent. No API key is stored in the app.

03

Draft, then you send

Outreach is drafts only. The product never sends; humans send. Export to CSV, or let your own agents query it through MCP.
