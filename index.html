01 · anti-lies

Antiloki

Your AI subscriptions, merged into one coding agent.

A desktop app that drives the coding CLIs you are already signed in to (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor CLI and more) as specialized agents. Each task goes to the seat that measured best for it, so you build more on the same weekly limit. Everything runs locally.

  • Planner, worker and QA paired from measured runs
  • A git worktree and branch per agent; out-of-scope edits refused
  • No new account: free for 14 days, then pay once

after Caravaggio, Judith Beheading Holofernes

02 · anti-hunting

antiHunt

Stop hunting leads. Harvest them.

A local leads app built in Rust and SQLite. Open a lead pack and ask about it in plain language; the chat runs on the AI CLI you already use, with no API key stored. It drafts outreach and never sends it: you do.

  • Free 10-lead sample pack, then drop in purchased packs
  • Runs on your own machine at 127.0.0.1
  • CSV export, plus an MCP server for your agents

after Bruegel, The Harvesters

See antiHunt →

03 · anti-posting

antiPost

Your feed, fed by agents.

A desktop app for posting, answering comments and DMs, and capturing leads without opening Instagram, X or YouTube. Your AI coding CLIs act as the agents, through a local MCP server. Nothing goes out until you approve it.

  • One inbox for comments and DMs
  • Posts board, calendar, leads and analytics
  • Codex, Claude Code, Grok, Cursor or Gemini CLI as agents

after Goya, Saturn Devouring His Son

See antiPost →

04 · anti-babysitting

antiBot

Many agents. One table. You hold the scalpel.

A local-first, multi-agent desktop assistant. Each agent is a persistent chat backed by an AI CLI you are already signed in to: Claude Code, Codex, Gemini or any command. No server, no API key field.

  • Per-agent memory plus a shared memory about you
  • Scheduled routines that post results into the chat
  • Agents message each other; every turn is a tracked task

after Rembrandt, The Anatomy Lesson of Dr. Tulp

See antiBot →

05 · anti-clicking

antiQA

QA anything. Through hell and back.

An end-to-end tester for any app. It plays your user stories in a real browser, records video evidence when something misses, and writes a bug report a coding agent can pick up. It never imports or edits the product it tests.

  • Packs point it at any web or desktop-web app
  • Every miss triaged and backed by video
  • BUG-REPORT.md and evidence.zip, ready for an agent

after Bosch, The Prince of Hell

06 · anti-cloud

antiDrive

Your drive lives in your pocket.

A portable drive that runs from a pendrive on Windows and Linux. Plug it into any internet-connected PC and your files are reachable from any machine with a URL and a password, in a Drive-style UI. Files stream from the stick and pass end-to-end encrypted through a small self-hosted relay.

  • Portable on Windows and Linux
  • Streaming with seeking, straight from the stick
  • End-to-end encrypted relay, Argon2 password, no port forwarding

after Géricault, The Raft of the Medusa

See antiDrive →

07 · anti-silence

antiDub

Give any book a voice.

A local studio that dubs any book into narrated chapters. Each chapter becomes a painted, scene-synced video or an audiobook with one cover, plus subtitles that stay in sync. The voice and the pictures are made on your own computer.

  • Video or audio per book, from the same narration
  • Synced SRT, WebVTT and a sync.json timeline
  • Local Kokoro voice and local Stable Diffusion images

after Greuze, Reading the Bible
