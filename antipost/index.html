How it works

Three steps. No new account.

01

Connect your channels

Instagram, X and YouTube come into one desktop app: an inbox, a posts list and board, leads, a calendar and analytics.

02

Bring your agents

Agents are coding CLIs you already pay for: Codex CLI, Claude Code, Grok CLI, Cursor Agent CLI or Gemini CLI. They talk to the app through a local MCP server.

03

Approve, then it goes out

Every reply and post waits for you. Nothing is published or sent until you approve it.
